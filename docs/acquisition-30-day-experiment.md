# BookSurf — first 30 days of acquisition

## Goal
Prove a repeatable, profitable channel for **Baja surf-trip requests** before scaling to other destinations. A lead is not a booking. Operator confirmation and payment are separate stages.

## First week: foundation
- Confirm Roger's experience inclusions, capacity, indicative pricing, cancellation terms, commission, photo rights and response SLA.
- Publish a clear Baja experience landing page with a real itinerary, what is included, operator disclosure, FAQs, and request-availability CTA.
- Verify conversion events and UTM/referral persistence end-to-end. Keep personal data out of analytics.
- Connect Search Console and submit sitemap; review indexing and page speed.
- Create a simple operator response playbook and an inquiry tracker.

## Week 2: channel tests
- Launch one tightly scoped Google Search campaign targeting high-intent Baja guided surf trips, with negative keywords and strict daily budget. Do not buy broad 'surf' traffic.
- Contact 10 relevant San Diego surf shops, coaches and surf communities with a specific referral offer; agree terms before issuing unique links.
- Ask Roger for authentic trip photography, reviews with permission, and one trip recap to use on the landing page.

## Week 3: optimize
- Review keyword-level lead quality and landing page conversion.
- Follow up on every request within a defined SLA; record response time, quote sent, accepted and payment status.
- Publish one useful guide answering real buyer questions: Baja surf-trip logistics, transport, skill fit and what is included. Avoid mass-generated thin SEO pages.

## Week 4: decide
- Scale the best-performing channel only if contribution margin after fulfillment, refunds and acquisition is positive.
- Pause channels producing low-quality inquiries.
- Expand to one additional partner or itinerary only once operations are repeatable.

## Funnel and metrics
Sessions -> availability requests -> qualified requests -> quotes -> payment links -> paid bookings -> completed trips -> repeat/referral.
Capture: first-touch UTM source/medium/campaign/content/term, referral code, landing path, timestamps, booking reference, operator, quote and gross profit. Do not send names or email to ad platforms.

## Unit economics
Contribution per booking = collected revenue - operator payout - payment fees - refund/chargeback allowance - variable support costs.
Maximum sustainable CAC < contribution per booking. Start with conservative assumptions and do not scale ads on unverified margin.

## Experiment budget (proposal, not spend authorization)
$300-$600 paid search over two weeks, plus direct partner outreach and organic content. Require user approval before enabling ads or incurring costs.

## Operating safeguards
No promise of availability before operator confirmation. No claim of specific surf conditions without sourced forecasts. No fake testimonials or inventory. Respect marketing consent and opt-outs. Maintain a partner referral ledger to avoid disputes.
